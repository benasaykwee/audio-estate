The neck

 
The gold dot is where the instrument decided to put your note. It is choosing a string and a fret the way a player would — staying near the last position, preferring an open string, and refusing to sound two notes on one string. Play a low E and then the F above it and watch the E stop: one string, one note. That rule is why this reads as a bass rather than a bass patch.

The hands

The attack layer

Drop a .wav here
or click to choose one — a real bass note, a pick attack, a thump
This is the seam between the three paths. The waveguide keeps doing the sustain; the sample you drop here supplies the attack, pitch-shifted to whatever note you play. Set String Level to zero and it becomes a plain sampler with the fingering brain still driving it. The root pitch is detected from the file, so you do not have to tell it what note you recorded — and it says what it found, so you can correct it if it is wrong.
